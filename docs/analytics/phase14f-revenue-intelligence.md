# Phase 14F — Executive Revenue Intelligence & Analytics

## Financial Metrics Engine
1. **Executive Metrics**: GMV, MRR, ARR, and blended take-rate modeling.
2. **Customer LTV Calculator**: Gross margin adjusted multi-year lifetime value formula.
3. **Churn Predictor**: Predictive ML heuristic scoring dormant days, support ticket frequency, and customer rating decay.

## APIs
- `POST /revenue-intelligence/executive-metrics`: Calculate GMV/MRR/ARR.
- `POST /revenue-intelligence/customer-ltv`: Calculate LTV.
- `POST /revenue-intelligence/churn-risk`: Predict customer churn risk.
