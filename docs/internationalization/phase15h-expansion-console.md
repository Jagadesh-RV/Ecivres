# Phase 15H — Enterprise Expansion Console

## Overview
Executive command dashboard monitoring active country market health, localized provider densities, country-level GMV/MRR revenue streams, and regulatory tax compliance audit statuses.

## Global Metrics Monitored
- **Active Markets Count**: 5 Live International Regions (`US`, `IN`, `GB`, `AE`, `DE`).
- **Global GMV & MRR Breakdown**: Country-by-country provider onboarding and billing tracking.
- **Compliance Health Monitor**: Real-time tax filing status, KYC completion percentage, and GDPR/data privacy compliance state.

## APIs
- `GET /expansion-console/overview`: Multi-country expansion analytics overview.
- `GET /expansion-console/compliance/:countryCode`: Individual country compliance audit report.
