# Sprint 15E — AI Workforce Automation

## Overview
The AI Workforce Automation system handles automated staff scheduling, leave balancing, overtime risk prediction, labor demand forecasting, and performance optimization.

## APIs
- `POST /workforce-ai-v2/schedules/optimize`: Optimize AI staff workforce schedule.
- `GET /workforce-ai-v2/organizations/:id/forecast`: Generate AI labor demand & staffing forecast.
